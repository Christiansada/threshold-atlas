# Community code of conduct

Participate with respect, patience, and curiosity. Critique ideas and code, not people. Welcome contributors with different backgrounds and experience.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable. Keep discussion relevant and assume good faith while taking reports of harm seriously.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions, warn participants, or restrict participation to protect the community. Decisions should be proportionate and explain the relevant behavior. Maintainers are held to the same standard.

Use GitHub's private abuse-reporting tools for sensitive conduct reports. Do not post personal details in public issues. For ordinary moderation questions that contain no sensitive information, contact maintainers in the relevant discussion.
